How MatchOracle Works
Our Hybrid V1 algorithm combines four specialised engines for maximum accuracy
A
Engine A — Match Prediction
Analyses team form, head-to-head records, home advantage, goals scored/conceded, and tactical matchups to predict win/draw/loss probabilities with percentage breakdowns.
B
Engine B — Player Rating
FIFA-style player rating system that evaluates goals, assists, appearances, position-specific metrics, and overall contribution to generate a 0–100 performance score.
D
Engine D — Monte Carlo Simulation
Runs 10,000+ match simulations using attack/defence ratings and ELO scores to generate the most likely scoreline, goal distributions, and outcome probabilities.
AI
Smart AI — Natural Language Interface
Ask any football question in plain English. Our Claude AI integration understands context, remembers your conversation, and provides detailed analysis with confidence ratings.
