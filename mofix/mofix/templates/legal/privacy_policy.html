Privacy Policy
Last updated: January 2025  ·  Effective immediately
Privacy Policy Terms of Service About Us
Your privacy matters to us. MatchOracle is committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how we use it. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use MatchOracle:

  • Account Information: Your email address, name, and password (stored as a secure hash) when you register.
  • Usage Data: Predictions you make, teams you analyse, and features you use within the platform.
  • Payment Information: Transaction references and payment status processed via Paystack. We do not store card numbers or banking details.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and session data for security and performance purposes.
  • Communications: Any messages you send to our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve the MatchOracle prediction service
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Send transactional emails (account confirmation, password resets, payment receipts)
  • Personalise your experience and track your prediction accuracy
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Analyse aggregate usage patterns to improve our AI models (anonymised data only)

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

  • Paystack: Our payment processor, for handling subscription payments securely.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Natural language queries may be processed by Claude AI. No personally identifiable information is included in AI requests.
  • Railway / Hosting: Our infrastructure provider stores your data on secure servers.
  • Legal authorities: If required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Bcrypt password hashing — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Secure session management with CSRF protection
  • Regular security reviews and dependency updates
  • Access controls limiting who can view user data

No system is 100% secure. If you suspect a security issue, please contact us immediately at privacy@matchoracle.com.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & Data Subject Rights)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and associated data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes.
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@matchoracle.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use:

  • Session cookies: To keep you logged in (expires after 30 days of inactivity).
  • CSRF cookies: To protect against cross-site request forgery attacks.
  • Local storage: To remember your PWA install preference and service worker state.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. payment records for tax purposes, retained for 7 years).

8. Children's Privacy

MatchOracle is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on the platform. Continued use of MatchOracle after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: privacy@matchoracle.com
  • Support: support@matchoracle.com
