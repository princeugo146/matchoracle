Our Mission

Democratising Football Intelligence

Football is the world's game — played in 200+ countries, watched by billions, and debated in every language. Yet for too long, the sophisticated data analysis used by professional clubs and broadcasters has been locked away from ordinary fans.

MatchOracle changes that. We've built a platform that puts professional-grade AI prediction tools in the hands of everyone — from the casual fan who wants to know if their team will win on Saturday, to the data analyst building the next generation of football insights.

Our Hybrid V1 algorithm combines statistical modelling, machine learning, and Claude AI to deliver predictions that are transparent, explainable, and continuously improving.

Our proprietary prediction engine combines ELO ratings, form analysis, head-to-head records, Monte Carlo simulation, and Claude AI reasoning into a single, unified intelligence system.

What We Offer

Everything You Need to Understand Football

Six powerful engines, one unified platform. MatchOracle gives you the full picture.

Engine A
⚽

Match Predictions

Get win/draw/loss probabilities for any match worldwide. Our Hybrid V1 algorithm analyses form, head-to-head records, home advantage, and dozens of other factors to give you the most accurate prediction possible.

Engine B
⭐

Player Ratings

Generate FIFA-style player ratings from real performance statistics. Input goals, assists, pass accuracy, defensive actions, and more to get a comprehensive 0–99 rating with detailed attribute breakdown.

Engine C

Team Rankings

Compare teams using our ELO-based ranking system. See how any two teams stack up against each other with head-to-head analysis, strength ratings, and historical performance trends.

Run thousands of Monte Carlo simulations to model every possible match outcome. Get expected goals, score distributions, and probability maps that go far beyond a simple win/loss prediction.
